# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is the repo owner during local development and manual playtesting. They use the app in a desktop browser while iterating on the game, testing whether the character-selection and conversation loop feels narratively interesting.

## Product Purpose

AI House is a browser-based narrative simulation game set in a modern apartment. Its current purpose is to validate the smallest entertaining loop: pick two characters, pick a room, set the opening speaker, enter a topic, and watch a short AI-driven conversation unfold. Success means the local prototype is easy to run, visually readable, and strong enough to support repeated playtesting and future phases.

## Brand Personality

Tense, theatrical, intimate. The product should feel like a strange domestic duel: readable and game-like, but with an undercurrent of unease. It should invite curiosity and replay rather than sterile tool-like efficiency.

## Anti-references

Do not let the interface feel like a generic SaaS dashboard, a flat prototype form, or a pastel AI demo. Avoid default purple-on-white palettes, weak visual hierarchy, novelty controls that hurt usability, and horror theming so heavy that the UI becomes muddy or unreadable.

## Design Principles

1. Make the player feel like they are staging a duel, not filling out a workflow form.
2. Keep the core loop instantly testable, especially for repeated local playtesting.
3. Let atmosphere support readability rather than compete with it.
4. Reveal conversation state clearly, so pacing and turn order are always obvious.
5. Preserve familiar interaction affordances even when the presentation becomes stylized.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for readable text and controls. Support keyboard-focus visibility, reduced-motion fallbacks, and clear status messaging during transcript playback. Preserve standard form semantics so the prototype remains usable during fast local iteration and debugging.
